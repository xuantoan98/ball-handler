**Full description**

```text
In Little Shadow Theatre, moving the lamp sends a tiny crowned shadow in the opposite direction. Study each miniature stage, guide the shadow around obstacles, and lead it through the moon door.

Explore 20 handcrafted puzzles. Activate star switches in order, avoid red sensors and moving hazards, slip around paper walls, and open doors before their time runs out.

Each attempt lasts up to 60 seconds. Preview a stage before playing, pause when needed, and return to your saved stage the next time you open the game. New stages unlock as you complete earlier ones.

Layered paper theatre art, warm lamplight, and a glowing mint moon door bring each puzzle to life. Switch between English and Vietnamese in Settings.

Move the lamp. Guide the shadow. Open the moon door.
```
